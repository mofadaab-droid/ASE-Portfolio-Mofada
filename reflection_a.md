# Reflection from Part A

Writing the concept analysis showed me that an AI agent is different from the programs I usually write. My Python and MATLAB projects follow fixed steps, but an agent decides its own actions, so I cannot predict everything it will do. That makes testing, limits on permissions, and human review much more important.

I also learned why Agile and version control fit this kind of work: when behavior is uncertain, small iterations and the ability to roll back keep mistakes manageable.
